Synonyms for a Law Book

If you mean "law in books" (a well-known legal concept introduced by jurist Roscoe Pound), it refers to the formal rules, statutes, and legal doctrines as they are officially written down in texts, codes, and court reports—contrasted with how laws are actually applied in real life ("law in action").

Rather than browsing general literature, enforcement agencies and legal practitioners consult the official statute book or legislative code. This structured compilation contains the codified laws, regulations, and ordinances enacted by governing bodies, ensuring that legal professionals always reference binding, written rules.
